# Atlanta Slip and Fall Lawyer

Stores, apartment complexes and other businesses have to keep their property reasonably safe. When they ignore a hazard and someone gets hurt, they can be responsible.

## Georgia at a glance

- **O.C.G.A. § 51-3-1**: owners must use ordinary care to keep premises safe for invited guests
- **Video**: store cameras are often recorded over, so ask for footage fast
- **2 years**: to file most injury lawsuits in Georgia (O.C.G.A. § 9-3-33)

## What Georgia law requires of property owners

Under O.C.G.A. § 51-3-1, an owner or occupier must use ordinary care to keep the premises and approaches safe for people invited onto the property, like customers and tenants. In most fall cases you have to show the owner knew or should have known about the hazard, and you did not.

## Common causes

- Spills and wet floors with no warning
- Broken stairs, missing handrails, uneven walkways
- Poor lighting in parking decks and stairwells
- Ice, leaks and drainage problems

## What to do now

1. Report the fall to the manager and ask for a copy of the incident report.
2. Take photos of what made you fall, before it's cleaned up or fixed.
3. Get names and numbers of witnesses.
4. Get medical care, and call us so we can ask for camera footage quickly.

## Questions

### What if I was partly at fault?

Georgia uses a modified comparative fault rule (O.C.G.A. § 51-12-33). You can still recover if you were less than 50% at fault, but your award is reduced by your share of the fault. At 50% or more, you recover nothing. Insurers know this, which is why they often try to shift blame onto the injured person.

### How long do I have to file an injury claim in Georgia?

For most injury claims, Georgia gives you **two years from the date of the injury** to file a lawsuit (O.C.G.A. § 9-3-33). Claims against a government are much shorter: written notice to a city is due within **six months** (O.C.G.A. § 36-33-5), and to a county or the State within **twelve months** (O.C.G.A. § 36-11-1 and § 50-21-26). Miss the deadline and the claim can be lost for good.
