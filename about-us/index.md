# About Avery Carnes Trial Lawyers

Avery Carnes is an Atlanta personal injury firm. Your case is always handled by an attorney, never only by a paralegal, case manager, customer service rep or investigator. The firm takes a limited number of cases each year.

## Thomas L. Carnes III (Trey Carnes), Founding Partner

Trey graduated from Shorter College and earned his law degree from Mississippi College School of Law in Jackson, Mississippi.

He began his legal career at a boutique firm in Cartersville, Georgia, representing riders hurt in motorcycle crashes.

Before opening his own practice, Trey represented Georgia hospitals and healthcare systems in third-party reimbursement. That work taught him how medical bills, provider liens and subrogation claims (an insurer's right to be paid back out of your recovery) shape what an injured person actually takes home.

He has represented injured people in Georgia, Alabama and Mississippi.

Outside the office, Trey lives in Brookhaven. He plays golf, runs, works on vintage vehicles, and goes to Braves, Falcons and Hawks games with his wife and son.

- Education: Shorter College, Mississippi College School of Law
- Memberships: State Bar of Georgia, American Bar Association, American Association for Justice, Georgia Trial Lawyers Association
- Admitted to practice: U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia; U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Georgia; Supreme Court of Georgia; Court of Appeals of Georgia; All State and Superior Courts in Georgia

## C. Benjamin Avery (Ben Avery), Founding Partner

Ben grew up in Gwinnett County and graduated from Buford High School. He earned his undergraduate degree at the University of Georgia and his law degree from John Marshall Law School.

After law school, Ben joined an insurance defense firm in Athens, Georgia, where he focused only on defending personal injury claims. He gained deep litigation experience there and tried cases to verdict.

He left that work to represent people instead of insurance companies. Today he represents only individuals and families who have been hurt by others.

Ben is an amateur golfer who competes in state and national amateur tournaments. He and his wife, Hannah, have three children: Amy, Will and Caroline.

- Education: University of Georgia, John Marshall Law School
- Memberships: State Bar of Georgia, American Bar Association, American Association for Justice, Georgia Trial Lawyers Association
- Admitted to practice: U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia; U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Georgia; Supreme Court of Georgia; Court of Appeals of Georgia; All State and Superior Courts in Georgia
