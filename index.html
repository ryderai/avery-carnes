Our Commitment

Your injury is personal to us.

We are committed to helping injured clients focus on healing and recovery. Whether you were hurt crossing the street or driving on the highway, every accident victim deserves the peace of mind that comes with experienced representation.

We have dedicated our practice to making the process of recovering full and fair compensation as effortless as possible for our clients.

And we do it on purpose with a limited caseload. We only accept a limited number of cases per year, so when you hire Avery Carnes, your case is handled by the attorneys themselves, not passed down to support staff.
