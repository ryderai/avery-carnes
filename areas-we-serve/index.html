About where we work

Do you take cases outside metro Atlanta?

Yes. We're based in Atlanta, but we take cases from every part of Georgia, from Savannah to Dalton. Both partners are admitted to practice in all of Georgia's state and superior courts. Call and tell us where it happened.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Atlanta and represent injured people across Georgia, in all 159 counties. Our office is at Perimeter Center. We have local guides for Fulton, DeKalb and Gwinnett counties and Sandy Springs, plus nine more metro Atlanta counties, from Bartow to Rockdale.

Can you come to me?

Yes. We come to you. Tell us where you are when you call.
