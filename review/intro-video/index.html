Your 15-second intro video

A short phone video of you saying hello. It goes on the homepage, right here. People trust a firm faster when they can see and hear the lawyers. We do all the editing, at no extra cost.

What to film

About 15 seconds. One clip from each of you, or one together. Pick a script below, or say it your own way. Keep it simple: who you are, who you help, and that the call is free.

Script

Trey

Hi, I’m Trey Carnes. Ben and I represent people hurt by someone else’s carelessness, anywhere in Georgia. If that’s you, call us. The first conversation is free.

Script

Ben

I’m Ben Avery. I’ve tried injury cases to verdict, and today I only represent people who’ve been hurt. If someone else’s carelessness hurt you, call us. It costs nothing to talk.

Script

Both together

TREY: Hi, I’m Trey Carnes. BEN: And I’m Ben Avery. TREY: If you’ve been hurt, we’d like to hear what happened. BEN: The call is free. Tell us what happened.

Georgia’s lawyer-advertising rules apply to video too: no promises about results and no “best” or “#1”. All three scripts are written to follow that, and the firm reads the final cut before it goes live.

How to film it on an iPhone

  1. Turn the phone sideways (landscape). The spot on the homepage is wide.
  2. Use the back camera, set to 4K at 30 fps (Settings → Camera → Record Video).
  3. Put the phone on a stand or stack of books at eye level, about an arm and a half away.
  4. Face a window. Light should hit your face, not come from behind you.
  5. Pick a quiet room. Turn off fans and close the door.
  6. Office or bookshelf background is perfect. Wear what you’d wear to meet a new client.
  7. Look at the camera lens, not the screen. Smile before you start talking.
  8. Wait two seconds, say the lines, wait two seconds, then stop. Do three takes.
  9. Reading off a card is fine. We cut out the pauses and the looks down.
  10. Send the original file (AirDrop, Google Drive or iCloud link). Texting it shrinks the quality.

What happens next

Send the clips to CJ. We trim them, add captions (so they work with the sound off), and place them on the homepage for your review before anything goes live.
